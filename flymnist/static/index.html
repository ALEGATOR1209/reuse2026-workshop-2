Click an action above to try it without the camera. Live, the fly does the action you recorded your gesture for. Each frame goes through the fly's eyes and 138k-neuron brain (50 ms of brain time, ~1 s to compute). A small decoder trained on your recordings reads the chosen neurons and decides what the fly does. Wing flapping is slowed down ~70× (a real fly beats its wings ~200 times a second), and the antennae move further than a real fly's. “CV” = accuracy on held-out stretches of your recordings.

Stick-figure gestures

The fly's eyes see a stick figure of your pose instead of the camera picture. A pose model in this browser finds your shoulders, arms and hands; the drawing goes through the fly brain on this device; decoders trained beforehand on other people's poses say which gesture it is. Try: right hand up, left hand up, both hands up, a hand heart 🫶 (at your chest or in front of your face), or hands down (rest).

Data and models used here

No photos are included: only stick figures drawn from body landmarks found in them, the fly brain's spike counts for those drawings, and decoders trained on them. Your data

On the published (static) version, your camera never leaves your device: frames, recordings and your trained decoder are kept in this browser only (delete them with “Clear recordings”).

With the “Stick figure” input, your camera picture never leaves your device either: MediaPipe finds your body in this browser, and only the stick figure, its spike counts and the body landmarks are kept as recordings (in this browser, or on the teacher's computer when the app runs from it). The MediaPipe library may send anonymous usage statistics to Google; it does not send your pictures.
